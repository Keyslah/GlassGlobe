Privacy Policy for Glass Globe

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Glass Globe is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

Glass Globe does not collect, store, or sell personal information.

The app may request access to your device location to accurately display the Earth, sky, and celestial objects, and access to your camera to provide augmented reality (AR) features. This information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Data Sharing

Glass Globe does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Analytics

Glass Globe does not use analytics services to track users.

Advertising

Glass Globe does not display third-party advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Glass Globe does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Because Glass Globe does not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is no user data stored by us.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the Glass Globe GitHub repository.
